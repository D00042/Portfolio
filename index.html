Monthly reports were assembled by hand — and stale by the time managers saw them.

Exports from SAP and HFM, cleaned in Excel, charts built manually, then emailed. Every correction restarted the process.

Context: shared-services finance team of a European travel group. Co-developed with one teammate.

A web app where the team enters data and reads live dashboards.

Data is managed in four categories. Role-based access lets a manager decide who sees which dashboard.
